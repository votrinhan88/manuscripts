Realistically, the accessibility to the training dataset and the teacher are two crucial factors for KD, that if missing, would render most KD methods inapplicable.
We propose a novel method to address this gap for distilling knowledge from a black-box teacher with no real images required.
Our framework DIP-KD leverages a synthesis pipeline to generate diverse synthetic images, then further improve image diversity with contrastive learning to perform soft KD.
We achieved state-of-the-art results on all eight benchmarks.
With a comprehensive study of our method, we hope to inspire new paradigms and challenges for future KD research.
