Knowledge distillation (KD) is a well-known technique for effectively transferring knowledge from a larger network (teacher) to a smaller network (student) with little sacrifice in performance.
However, most KD methods require full access to the teacher network and even its original training set, which are impractical due to intellectual property or security concerns.
These difficulties have originated a more challenging problem known as \textit{black-box data-free KD}, which requires only the teacher's top-1 predictions and without any original data.
While recent approaches have yet to leverage diversity, we propose Diverse Image Priors Knowledge Distillation ({\methodname}) with twofold novelties: we first synthesize \textit{image priors} --- semantically diverse synthetic images, then we further optimize them for diversity via contrastive learning.
Through extensive experiments, we achieve state-of-the-art results among other black-box data-free KD methods on eight image benchmarks with thorough ablation studies.
